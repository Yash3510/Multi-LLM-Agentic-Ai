# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately through GitHub: the repository's **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**. Please don't open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version or commit you tested.

## What we treat as security-critical

- the evidence ledger and its signatures
- sign-off and approvals
- the policy engine and data permissions
- the sandbox
- pack and skill signing and installation
- anything that sends data off the machine
